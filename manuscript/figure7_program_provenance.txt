Preprocessing:
- Counts and metadata CSV.gz files are preferred over the 10x ZIP.
- Metadata cell barcodes are matched to count-matrix cell barcodes.
- Sample labels are parsed from the metadata sample column.
- AD, DLB, PSP treated samples are retained as disease conditions.
- UT_C57 and UT_MAPT untreated controls are retained only to compute treatment-minus-untreated contrast features.
- No cell is used as an independent classifier sample.
- Cell-level counts are aggregated into biological sample/treatment-level pseudobulk profiles.
- Pseudobulk profiles are library-size normalized as counts per 10,000 and log1p transformed.
- Top variable gene expression features, curated module scores, global inspection PCA columns, and untreated-contrast features are written.
- No clear cell-type annotation was detected in the available metadata, so all relevant cells were used and this warning was logged.
- During model evaluation, global GEO PCA columns are excluded; fold-local PCA is fit inside training folds only from GEO expression columns.

Matched-sample status:
- Exact tau/GEO sample IDs did not match.
- The merge did not create artificial one-to-one sample matches.
- GEO features were summarized by disease label and joined as disease-level auxiliary features.
- The merged table records this as geo_auxiliary_level=disease_mean.

Evaluation protocol:
- 5-fold StratifiedGroupKFold.
- Groups are biological tau replicate IDs.
- The training script asserts that no group appears in both train and validation folds.
- Imputation, scaling, and fold-local GEO PCA are fit on training folds only.
