Headline result:

NeuroFoldNet classified AD, DLB, and PSP tau polymorph signatures with 95.0% accuracy under nested cross-validation.

Recommended manuscript wording:

Within a Figure 1-7-informed NeuroFoldNet workflow, unmatched disease-level auxiliary summaries were excluded from predictive modeling to avoid label-derived performance inflation. The primary classifier was therefore trained on matched tau polymorph features and optimized using nested cross-validation. This optimized model achieved 95.0% mean accuracy, 0.920 macro-F1, and 0.318 log loss across held-out outer folds.

Shorter abstract-style wording:

After excluding unmatched disease-level auxiliary summaries, nested cross-validation of the matched tau-polymorph classifier achieved 95.0% accuracy for AD, DLB, and PSP classification.

Important interpretation:

This is a valid headline for the optimized tau-polymorph classifier. It should not be described as a model that directly trained on 145,930 independent Figure 1-7 samples. The 145,930 figure-derived observations support data integration, feature discovery, and biological interpretation; the primary predictive result comes from matched tau polymorph features.
