Figure 7 single-cell RNA-seq data from GSE277105 were incorporated as auxiliary transcriptomic features to extend the tau polymorph feature space with disease-associated neuronal response information. Raw cell-level expression profiles were not treated as independent disease-classification samples. Instead, cell barcodes were matched to metadata, filtered to AD, DLB, PSP tau oligomer exposure conditions with untreated controls retained only for contrast estimation, and aggregated into biological sample/treatment-level pseudobulk profiles prior to modeling.

Expression profiles were library-size normalized, log-transformed, and summarized using compact variable-gene, gene-module, and treatment-minus-untreated contrast features. Where cell-type annotations were available, excitatory neuronal annotations were prioritized; in their absence, all relevant cells were aggregated with this limitation recorded. The resulting transcriptomic summaries were merged with the original tau polymorph features without replacing any existing morphology, proteolytic resistance, seeding, or electrophysiology measurements.

Model evaluation remained replicate-aware. Cross-validation was performed using stratified group-aware folds, with explicit checks that no biological replicate group appeared in both training and validation partitions. Fold-sensitive preprocessing steps, including imputation, scaling, and dimensionality reduction of GEO expression features, were fit only within training folds before being applied to held-out validation samples. Thus, Figure 7 data were used as auxiliary biological features while avoiding cell-level pseudoreplication and preserving evaluation on held-out real data.
